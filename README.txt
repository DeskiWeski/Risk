EASTLAKE TURF WARS
==================
A Risk-style turf war on a map of Eastlake, Ohio. Hire goons, send black SUVs
to the block, shoot it out, take the turf. Play solo vs the computer, or host
a game from your phone and have friends join on theirs.

PUT IT ONLINE (GitHub Pages, from your phone)
1. In the GitHub app/site, create a new public repository (any name).
2. Add file > Upload files > upload index.html. Commit.
3. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder / (root). Save.
4. After a minute your game is at https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPONAME/

HOST A GAME
- Open the link, tap HOST ONLINE GAME. You get a 4-letter room code and a link.
- Tap COPY LINK or SHARE and text it to friends. They open it and join.
- Tap a seat to cycle: waiting for friend / computer / closed. Then START GAME.
- Keep the host's phone awake and the page open. If a friend drops, a computer takes their seat.
- Needs an internet connection (uses free PeerJS signaling).

THE GANGS (cartoon versions)
Bandit - SS4L (orange), Hauncho - SS4L (green), Boggy - EST (blue), BJ - Willoway (red).

HOW TO PLAY
- Each turn: collect cash from your turfs (plus district bonuses). Goons cost $100.
- Tap your turf to hire a goon, or AUTO-HIRE ALL. DONE HIRING moves on.
  It also moves on by itself when you run out of cash.
- Attack: just tap an enemy turf next to yours. Your strongest neighbor goes in.
  (Or tap your own turf first to choose the attacker.)
- The battle runs by itself: SUVs pull up side by side, goons get out and
  shoot. Dice decide each round (you roll up to 3, defender up to 2, highest
  dice compare, ties go to the defender). RETREAT is your only choice mid-fight.
- Win and the turf is taken automatically. Tap another enemy turf to keep going,
  or END ATTACK. After attacking, move goons between your turfs or END TURN.
  The game skips steps that have nothing to do.

ARMORY (hire phase, buttons above the bottom bar)
- RPG $200: blows up an enemy goon on the first roll of your next battle.
- TANK $500: rolls in and adds a die to every roll. Survives if you win.
- HELICOPTER $900: gunship strafes the first 2 rolls, 1 kill each.
Items are used in the next battle you fight (attacking or defending), and show up in the scene.
Capturing a wiped-out gang takes its cash and unused items.

STREET NEWS: random events like cops on Lake Shore, Pierogi Night, Walmart Rollback, Route 91 pileups.
KEY shows district bonuses. TALK sends trash talk online.
